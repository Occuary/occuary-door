What members can do that listings cannot.

Agent IQ / Agent Audit

Run an audit on the agent in front of you. Track record. Similar homes. How they get paid. What paperwork they want signed. Where their incentives can drift from yours. You keep the leverage of knowing their value before they price yours.

Independent title ordering

Order title yourself. Compare providers. Keep the choice. Nobody on this platform is paid to steer you to a title company. If a firm wants that choice, they can ask. You do not have to give it away.

On-demand intelligence

Ask the real questions when they show up. Showing rules. Offer structure. What a fee means in dollars. What a document is asking you to give up. You do not need to book a listing appointment to think.

AI negotiation assistant

Use AI, and an optional virtual assistant, to interview agents and negotiate terms on your behalf. Same questions every time. Fees in dollars. Paperwork first. You approve the outcome. The assistant does not get a cut of the house.

Privacy first

Your identity, financial details, and IP address are treated as part of the deal, not as marketing inventory. The traditional process collects you early because collected buyers are easier to move. This membership is built to keep you uncollected until you choose otherwise.

Local vendors. No kickbacks.

Inspectors. Title companies. Closing support and related local services. Members get introductions to a vetted hyper-local list. Occuary does not take a referral fee for the introduction. If a vendor pays anyone to be recommended, they do not belong on this list.

Straight answers

Do I still need an agent?

You can hire one. You can fire one. You can wait. Occuary does not take that choice from you. It stops you from making it blind.

Do you represent me in the transaction?

No. We are not your broker and we are not your lawyer. We give you intelligence, scripts, audits, vendor choice, and assistant tools. You stay the decision maker.

Why would title be independent?

Because title is part of your money and your file. If the person helping you buy the house also benefits when a certain title company is used, you should know that before you say yes. Here, you pick first.
